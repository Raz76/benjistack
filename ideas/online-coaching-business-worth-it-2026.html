What coaches are actually complaining about

So — is it worth it for you?

Demand for coaching is real and growing. But the data is clear about what separates coaches who get paying clients from the ones who don't:

  • Get specific about who you help and how. "Invisible" is what a generic, credentialed-but-unclear positioning looks like in a saturated market.
  • Practice saying exactly how you help. The nerve to state it plainly is repeatedly named as the actual bottleneck, not the skill itself.
  • Build a real month-one retention system. Losing clients right after onboarding is an engagement gap you can design around, not bad luck.
  • Systematize delivery early, even six-figure coaches are still stuck manually handling every check-in. Fix it before you scale, not after.
  • If you have no audience, go direct. Outreach and existing-network conversations are what start the pipeline, not waiting to be discovered.
